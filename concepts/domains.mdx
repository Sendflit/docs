Every email you send is signed with a sending domain you control. Receiving servers check the signature and the published DNS records to decide whether to trust your mail, so verification comes first.

## What SendFlit checks

| Record | Host | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| DKIM (`TXT`) | `sendflit._domainkey.yourdomain.com` | Signs your mail with a 2048-bit key unique to your domain. |
| SPF (`TXT`) | `yourdomain.com` | Authorizes SendFlit to send for your domain. |
| DMARC (`TXT`) | `_dmarc.yourdomain.com` | Sets your policy for failed checks and where aggregate reports go. |

Sending from a domain that isn't verified returns `403`.

## Add a domain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register the domain">
    ```bash
    curl -X POST https://api.sendflit.com/v1/domains \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer $SENDFLIT_KEY" \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{"name": "yourdomain.com"}'
    ```
    The response includes the domain `id` and the records to publish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the DNS records">
    Add each `TXT` record at your DNS provider. Changes can take time to propagate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    ```bash
    curl -X POST https://api.sendflit.com/v1/domains/<domain_id>/verify \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer $SENDFLIT_KEY"
    ```
    Verification reads the live DNS records. Run it again if the records aren't visible yet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
If your DNS is in Amazon Route 53, you can connect AWS credentials once in the dashboard and SendFlit publishes the records for you.
</Tip>

## List and inspect domains

| Method | Path | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `GET` | `/v1/domains` | List your domains. |
| `GET` | `/v1/domains/{id}/records` | The DNS records to publish for one domain. |
| `GET` | `/v1/domains/{id}/deliverability` | Deliverability status for one domain. |
| `POST` | `/v1/domains/{id}/verify` | Check DNS and verify. |

## Subdomains

Use a subdomain, such as `mail.yourdomain.com`, to keep transactional mail separate from your main domain's reputation. Register the subdomain as its own domain, then publish its records.
